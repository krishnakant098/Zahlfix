2. Website access and server logs

When you visit this website, technically necessary connection data may be processed, including your IP address, time of access, requested resources, browser information and status codes. These data are used to deliver the website securely, investigate errors and prevent misuse.

3. Password-protected access

Following successful PIN entry, the website uses a technically necessary session cookie for access control. The cookie does not contain the PIN and is set with HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Strict attributes. It is not retained for a persistent 'remember me' function; normally it ends with your browser session, and an additional server-side expiry applies. In production it is transmitted over HTTPS only.

To limit automated PIN attacks, failed login attempts are temporarily evaluated. The application uses a cryptographic hash of the IP address to maintain counters in Netlify Blobs. These counters are cleared following a successful login and attempts outside the evaluation window are disregarded. Netlify may also apply infrastructure-level rate limiting.

4. Contact and enquiry form

The enquiry form requests your name, company, work email, optional phone number and a description of your project or challenge. In the current version, submitting the form opens an email draft in your own email application; the website does not save the form fields in its own database. If you send the email to us, we process your information to respond to your enquiry and, where applicable, take steps requested before entering into a contract.

5. External images

The website may load images from Pexels. Loading these files may establish a technical connection to Pexels. Images can be served locally for a more privacy-conscious production setup.

6. Your rights

Subject to the GDPR and its conditions, you may have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and objection. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.

7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. Password protection does not provide end-to-end encryption for sensitive files. Please do not send particularly sensitive or confidential documents over unsecured contact channels.
